## What This Module Teaches

Change management isn't about manipulation or overcoming resistance through force. It's about understanding human dynamics and working with them, not against them.

### Key Concepts

**The Human Side of Change**
- Loss and grief in transitions
- The gap between logical and emotional responses
- Why good changes face resistance
- Change saturation and fatigue

**Change Readiness**
- Assessing whether conditions support change
- Building foundation before pushing change
- Timing and sequencing matters

**Communication and Engagement**
- Telling isn't selling
- Addressing "What's in it for me?"
- Creating dialogue, not monologue
- Different messages for different stakeholders

**Resistance as Information**
- What resistance tells you
- Valid concerns vs. habit
- Working with resistance, not against it

**The Messy Middle**
- Change is not linear
- Maintaining momentum through difficulty
- Supporting people through transition
- Adjusting as you learn

**Making Change Stick**
- New habits and patterns
- Reinforcing mechanisms
- Preventing backsliding
- Declaring victory too early

## Common Breakthroughs

- "I've been treating resistance as the problem when it's actually information"
- "I announced change without preparing people for it"
- "We're forcing change faster than people can actually adapt"
- "I need to address the emotional side, not just the logical side"
- "Some changes shouldn't happen - this resistance is valid"
