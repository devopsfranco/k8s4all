## What This Module Teaches

Learning isn't an event - it's a continuous practice woven into how you work.

### Key Concepts

**Learning vs. Training**
- Acquiring knowledge vs. changing behavior
- Formal education vs. learning from experience
- Both matter, experience often teaches more

**Integrated Learning**
- Learning through doing
- Reflection in action
- Making learning part of work, not separate

**Adaptive Learning**
- Unlearning and relearning
- Updating mental models
- Embracing uncertainty

**Learning From Experience**
- Reflection practices
- After-action reviews
- Learning from success and failure
- Building better intuition

**Organizational Learning**
- Individual vs. collective learning
- Knowledge sharing
- Learning culture
- Systems that learn
