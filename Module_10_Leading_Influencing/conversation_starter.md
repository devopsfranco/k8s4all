## What This Module Teaches

Leadership isn't about titles or charisma. It's about helping others succeed, creating conditions for good work, and having positive impact.

### Key Concepts

**Leadership vs. Management**
- Setting direction vs. executing
- Inspiring vs. coordinating
- Both matter, different skills

**Influence Without Authority**
- Building relationships
- Credibility and trust
- Understanding motivations
- Creating win-win situations

**Development and Growth**
- Coaching mindset
- Effective delegation
- Constructive feedback
- Creating learning opportunities

**Authentic Leadership**
- Values-aligned action
- Self-awareness
- Vulnerability and strength
- Finding your style

**Navigating Complexity**
- Power dynamics
- Organizational politics
- Difficult conversations
- Ethical leadership
