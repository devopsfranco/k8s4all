# Module 10: Leading and Influencing

## Overview

Leadership is creating positive change and helping others succeed. This module helps you lead and influence effectively in your context.

## Learning Objectives

1. Influence without formal authority
2. Develop and coach others
3. Build trust and credibility
4. Navigate difficult interpersonal situations
5. Find your authentic leadership approach
6. Create positive impact in your sphere

## Time Commitment

**60-90 minutes**
