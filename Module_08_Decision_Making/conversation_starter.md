## What This Module Teaches

Good decision-making isn't about always being right - it's about having a sound process that accounts for uncertainty and learns over time.

### Key Concepts

**Decision Quality vs. Outcome Quality**
- Good process ≠ good outcome (luck matters)
- Bad process can have good outcomes (temporarily)
- Focus on process, accept outcome uncertainty

**Frameworks for Different Decision Types**
- One-way vs. two-way door decisions
- High-stakes vs. low-stakes
- Individual vs. collaborative decisions
- Fast decisions vs. deliberate decisions

**Handling Uncertainty**
- What you can know vs. what's unknowable
- Probabilistic thinking
- Scenario planning
- Building in reversibility

**Decision Traps**
- Confirmation bias
- Sunk cost fallacy
- Analysis paralysis
- Anchoring
- Group think

**Learning From Decisions**
- Pre-mortems and post-mortems
- Decision journals
- Separating process from outcome
- Updating beliefs with new evidence
