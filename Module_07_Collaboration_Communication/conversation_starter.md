## What This Module Teaches

Collaboration and communication are foundational skills that amplify or undermine everything else you do.

### Key Concepts

**Communication Clarity**
- Purpose, audience, context
- Signal vs. noise
- Understanding vs. agreement

**Active Listening**
- Hearing to understand, not to respond
- Asking questions that reveal understanding
- Navigating assumptions

**Coordination Without Bureaucracy**
- Just enough structure
- Visibility and transparency
- Asynchronous and synchronous balance

**Productive Conflict**
- Disagreement as information
- Addressing issues early
- Separating people from problems

**Communication Patterns**
- Rhythms and cadences
- Appropriate channels
- Documentation philosophy
