# Quick Start Guide

## New to Agentic Upskilling? Start Here.

### What is this?

This is a **personalized learning framework** that uses AI conversation to help you:
- Understand concepts that matter to YOUR situation
- Apply ideas to YOUR real challenges
- Think critically about YOUR context
- Build skills through dialogue, not memorization

### How is this different from other courses?

**Traditional Courses:**
- Fixed content for everyone
- Generic examples
- Learn first, apply later (maybe)
- Passive consumption

**Agentic Upskilling:**
- Content adapts to YOU
- Your real situations and challenges
- Apply while you learn
- Active conversation and discovery

### What do I need?

1. **Access to an AI assistant** (Claude, ChatGPT, or similar)
2. **30-60 minutes** to start
3. **Curiosity** and willingness to think
4. **Honesty** about your situation and challenges

No technical background required. No specific job role required.

### How does it work?

1. **Each module has a conversation starter prompt**
   - Copy it and paste into your AI conversation
   - The AI asks you questions about your situation
   - You answer honestly and specifically

2. **The AI adapts to your context**
   - Explains concepts in terms relevant to you
   - Uses your real examples
   - Helps you discover insights
   - Guides you to apply what you learn

3. **You move at your own pace**
   - No deadlines
   - No grades
   - No fixed path
   - Complete modules when they're relevant to you

- The AI will ask about your current situation
- You'll reflect on your role, challenges, and goals
- Takes 30-60 minutes
- No wrong answers - just honest exploration

**Subsequent modules:**
- Build on your specific context from Module 00
- Dive deeper into particular challenges
- Learn frameworks by applying them to your real situations
- Each module: 45-120 minutes

### Tips for success

**Be specific, not generic**
- Bad: "I work in business"
- Good: "I manage a team of 5 people in a marketing department at a mid-size software company"

**Use real examples**
- Don't make up hypotheticals
- Use actual situations you've faced
- The more real, the more valuable the learning

**Push back when needed**
- If something doesn't apply to you, say so
- If an explanation doesn't make sense, ask for a different one
- You're in control of the conversation

**Take your time**
- Pause between questions to think
- You can end and resume conversations anytime
- Learning happens through reflection, not rushing

**Apply immediately**
- Try out ideas in your real work
- Come back and discuss what happened
- Use insights to inform decisions

### Common questions

**Q: I'm not technical. Is this for me?**

A: Yes! This framework is designed for non-technical audiences. Concepts are explained in plain language.

**Q: Do I have to do all the modules?**

A: No. Do the ones relevant to you. Skip or reorder as needed.

**Q: How long does this take?**

A: As long as you want. Some people work through several modules in a week. Others take months. Both are fine.

**Q: What if I don't like talking to AI?**

A: You can use the prompts as reflection exercises, work through them with a colleague, or have a coach facilitate.

**Q: What if the AI doesn't understand my situation?**

A: Redirect it. Say "That doesn't fit my context because..." or "Let me clarify..." The AI will adapt.

**Q: Is there a certificate or credential?**

A: No. The value is in what you learn and apply, not in a certificate.
