## What This Module Teaches

Good measurement illuminates reality. Bad measurement distorts it.

### Key Concepts

**Outcomes vs. Outputs vs. Activities**
- What you achieve vs. what you produce vs. what you do
- Measuring impact, not just busywork

**Leading vs. Lagging Indicators**
- Signals that predict future vs. report past
- Balancing both types

**Goodhart's Law**
- "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure"
- Avoiding metric gaming

**Qualitative and Quantitative Signals**
- Numbers and narratives
- Data and stories
- Both matter

**The Right Amount of Measurement**
- Too little: Flying blind
- Too much: Analysis paralysis
- Just right: Clear signals, minimal overhead
