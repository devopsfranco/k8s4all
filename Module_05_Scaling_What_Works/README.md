# Module 05: Scaling What Works

## Overview

Taking something from small success to large impact requires more than "doing more." This module helps you understand what changes at scale and how to prepare for it.

## Core Concept

**Multiplication**: Just do more (rarely works)
**Scaling**: Adapt for larger context while preserving what makes it work

## Learning Objectives

1. Identify what's truly scalable vs. what's context-dependent
2. Prepare successes for expansion
3. Anticipate scaling challenges before they appear
4. Maintain quality and culture during growth
5. Make strategic choices about what to scale vs. protect
6. Handle different challenges at different scales

## Why This Matters

Scaling done well: Impact multiplies, systems work without you, success spreads

Scaling done poorly: Quality degrades, culture dilutes, what worked stops working
